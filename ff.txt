# 1. CORE PRODUCT POSITIONING

Do NOT position Execution Authority Gate as merely another fraud classifier.

Do NOT replace the existing concept with a generic fraud-detection dashboard.

The central thesis must become:

> **Fraud detection determines risk. Execution authority determines whether the action can actually happen.**

The primary product narrative:

> **Discover the attack. Simulate it. Detect it. Decide whether it is authorized to execute. Prove what happened.**

The final system should visibly demonstrate:

```text
IDENTIFY
   ↓
GENERATE
   ↓
DETECT
   ↓
AUTHORIZE
   ↓
EXECUTE / BLOCK
   ↓
PROVE
```

The key differentiation:

> **A probabilistic AI system may recommend an action. It does not automatically acquire authority to execute that action.**

Execution Authority Gate is the deterministic execution-authority boundary.
